02Chunk pack — say these until they're automatic 10 min

Fluent speakers don't build sentences word by word. They reach for ready-made openings. Play each line, repeat it out loud three times, then say it once with your own ending.

03Shadowing — speak at the same time as the voice 10 min

Play it slow first and speak along with it, half a beat behind — don't wait for it to finish. Then take it up to full speed. This is the single fastest way to fix rhythm.

—

04Now you speak 15 min

Start the timer, then answer out loud until it runs out. Tap the counter every time you catch yourself saying “uh” — noticing it is most of the cure. Keep going through mistakes; never restart a sentence. None of this needs a microphone.
